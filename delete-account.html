Account & Data Deletion

SoloForm for Android · Last updated: 11 November 2026

If you have a SoloForm account, you can request deletion of your account and associated information. You do not have to install the app again to make a request.

How to request deletion

  1. From SoloForm: If you can sign in, go to More → Account deletion → Request account deletion and submit the request.
  2. Without the app: Email soloform.support@gmail.com with the subject SoloForm Account Deletion Request. Use the email address associated with your SoloForm account if possible. Do not send passwords, ID documents or health records.

We may ask for reasonable verification to protect the account from unauthorized deletion. Requests are currently processed manually; tapping the in-app request button does not immediately erase the account.

Information deleted

Once the request is verified and completed, we will delete the Firebase Authentication account and app-controlled records associated with it, including applicable closed beta entitlement data and deletion-request records, except information that must be retained for lawful reasons. Local exercise records, measurements, schedules and manually exported backup files may be stored on your device or in a storage service you selected, and are not necessarily removed remotely.

Remove local and backup data

To remove local SoloForm data from a device, use Android Settings → Apps → SoloForm → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall SoloForm. Review any exported JSON backups, copies in Google Drive and Android system backups separately. Deleting a Google account or uninstalling the app alone does not necessarily submit a Firebase account deletion request.

Processing and retention

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. Limited records may be retained only where required by applicable law, security or dispute resolution, and only for as long as necessary for that purpose. If more information or time is needed, we will contact you.

Questions

Contact soloform.support@gmail.com.

You can request deletion even if SoloForm's closed beta has ended.
