Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · App: SoloForm (Android) · Developer: SoloForm

SoloForm is an Android fitness application that helps people plan workouts, track exercise sessions, manage training schedules, and review progress. This Privacy Policy explains how SoloForm handles information when you use the application, including its closed beta.

1. Information we handle

  • Account and sign-in information. When you sign in with Google or email and password, Firebase Authentication processes your account identifier (UID), email address, authentication information and, where available, basic Google profile details such as your display name. We do not ask you to give us your Google password.
  • Workout, fitness and profile information. You may enter fitness goals, equipment preferences, workout plans, exercise logs, weights, repetitions, calendar entries, measurements and progress information. These records are primarily stored locally on your device and associated with your signed-in account within that installation. SoloForm does not currently provide a verified, automatic cross-device cloud sync of all workout records.
  • Beta programme information. For closed beta access, Cloud Firestore may store your authenticated UID, first eligible sign-in time and beta entitlement status. A separate administrative control determines whether beta access is open and when it ends.
  • Account deletion requests. If you request deletion in the app, a request associated with your account may be recorded in Cloud Firestore for manual processing.
  • Optional Health Connect data. Only when you grant the relevant Android permissions, SoloForm may read height and weight from Health Connect for your profile and write a workout session summary to Health Connect. Health Connect is optional; you can enter information manually and revoke permission in your device settings.
  • Technical data. Google Firebase and Google Play services may process technical and security information, such as device and network details, to provide authentication, app distribution and abuse prevention, subject to their applicable policies.

2. How information is used

We use information to authenticate your account; operate and personalize workout planning, tracking and fitness features; enforce beta eligibility and trial limits; maintain account security; respond to support and deletion requests; and comply with applicable legal obligations. Health Connect information is used only for the fitness functions for which you grant permission.

3. Local data, backups and data sharing

Workout records are primarily saved on your device. If you deliberately export a backup, choose a file location, upload it to cloud storage, or share a graphic or video, you control the destination and the recipient. SoloForm does not automatically post your workouts or media to social networks.

Depending on your Android device and Google backup settings, eligible local app data may also be included in encrypted Android system backups or device-to-device transfers. Android backup is distinct from a SoloForm-managed cloud synchronization service. You can manage Android backups through your device/Google account settings.

4. Third-party services

SoloForm currently uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to support sign-in and beta access, Google Sign-In for optional authentication, and Google Play for app distribution. Optional Health Connect access is mediated by Android permissions. These services may process data under their own applicable terms and privacy notices.

SoloForm's closed beta does not include an advertising SDK or active paid subscriptions. We do not sell personal data or use Health Connect information for advertising.

5. Security

We use authentication and access controls for Firebase services, including rules intended to restrict access to beta records. Data sent to Google Firebase services uses secure network connections. No security measure or local device storage is completely risk-free. Keep your device and Google account protected and avoid publicly sharing backup files containing your fitness records.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored information generally remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data or uninstall the app, subject to device backup settings. Account data, beta entitlement records and any account deletion request stored in Firebase may remain until the account is deleted and associated records are processed. We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days, except where limited retention is required by law, security or dispute-resolution obligations. We will inform you if a request requires additional verification or time.

You may request deletion using SoloForm → More → Account deletion → Request account deletion, where available, or by emailing soloform.support@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. Deletion is currently processed manually and is not instant. For steps and the types of information affected, see Account & Data Deletion.

If you exported copies or used Android/third-party backups, you may need to delete those copies separately from the relevant device or service.

7. Your choices

  • Use Google Sign-In or email/password authentication where available.
  • Skip Health Connect and manage or revoke its permissions in Android settings.
  • Choose whether to enable notifications and share/export workout data.
  • Request account and associated data deletion as explained above.

8. Children

SoloForm is designed as a general fitness tool and is not specifically directed at children. Do not provide another person's personal or sensitive information without authorization.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy if SoloForm's features, data practices or legal requirements change. The latest effective date will appear at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated where appropriate.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, data access concerns or account deletion, email soloform.support@gmail.com.

Closed beta notice. SoloForm is in testing. Some features may change before the official release. This policy must be updated before activating any materially new data collection, advertising or subscription features.
